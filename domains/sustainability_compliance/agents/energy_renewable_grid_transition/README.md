# ESG: Renewable Energy & Grid Transition Agent

An enterprise AI agent for **ESG: Renewable Energy & Grid Transition**, built with Google ADK for Gemini Enterprise.

---

## Why This Agent Matters

Retail executives and ESG leaders require real-time visibility into sustainability metrics, regulatory disclosures, and operational compliance to achieve net-zero targets, avoid statutory penalties, and enhance brand equity. This agent unifies internal operational telemetry (emissions, waste, renewable energy, supplier audits) with external market intelligence and global environmental standards.

---

## Key Metrics Tracked

| Metric | Business Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Renewable Energy Share %** | Percentage of total retail electricity demand met by renewables |
| **On-Site Solar Generation (MWh)** | Total megawatt-hours generated by rooftop solar arrays |
| **Peak Demand Cost Savings $** | Demand charge savings from battery dispatch & load shaving |
| **Avoided Grid Costs $** | Utility bill savings from clean power generation |

---

## What It Answers & Sub-Agent Routing

The orchestrator routes user questions to specialized sub-agents:

- **Internal BigQuery Data (`data_insights`)**:
  - On-site solar rooftop generation MWh, corporate PPA renewable contract performance, peak demand shaving events, or battery storage kWh arbitrage savings
- **External Market Context (`market_context`)**:
  - Commercial solar PPA tariff trends, corporate renewable energy buyer association (CEBA) benchmarks, federal clean energy tax credits (IRA), or battery storage cost curves
- **Synthesized Responses**:
  - Blends internal performance data with external market trends, standards, and benchmarks.

## Sample Q&A Showcase

### 1. Internal BigQuery Data Insights
**User**: *What is our total on-site solar energy generation in MWh and avoided utility cost in 2026 YTD?*  
**Agent**: According to energy management meter logs (`esgc_ergt_onsite_solar_generation`), our rooftop solar installations across DC-101, Store 101, and Store 102 generated **2,100.5 MWh of clean electricity** in 2026 YTD (January through June). This provided **$283,575 in direct avoided grid utility costs**. Combined with our contracted virtual PPAs (108,000 MWh annual commitment), renewable power now covers **68.3% of total enterprise electrical load**.

### 2. External Market Grounding
**User**: *What are current market price trends for corporate virtual Power Purchase Agreements (vPPAs) for solar and wind in ERCOT and PJM?*  
**Agent**: According to the Clean Energy Buyers Association (CEBA) and LevelTen Energy 2026 index, P25 contract prices for corporate solar vPPAs average $34–$38/MWh in ERCOT and $40–$45/MWh in PJM. Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) investment tax credits and domestic content bonuses have stabilized long-term contract pricing, with corporate off-takers favoring 10-to-15 year indexed structures with settlement caps.

### 3. Visual Analytics Artifact
**User**: *Show a chart comparing monthly on-site solar generation across our retail distribution centers.*  
**Agent**: Rendered chart artifact `sample_chart.png` illustrating performance metrics.
